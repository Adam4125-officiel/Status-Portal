Scheduled tasks

Recurring background jobs this portal runs on its own. Each one can be turned off, rescheduled, or run immediately without waiting for its next slot. A task that fails is recorded here and retried on its next scheduled run — it never stops the other tasks, and never takes the portal down with it. The scheduler looks for due tasks every {{ tick_seconds }}s, so that's the finest granularity a schedule can actually achieve.
