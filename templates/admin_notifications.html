Notification channels

Where this portal sends you a push when something happens: an incident opening, updating or resolving, a maintenance window starting or ending, and a disk crossing the low-space threshold. Every channel is optional and they all fire together — a channel that fails is logged and skipped, and never holds up the check that triggered it.

Email recipients

Who gets incident, maintenance and low-disk alerts by email. Comma-separated for several, the same format as the Discord ID lists elsewhere. Unlike the rest of the SMTP settings this lives in the database, so changing it takes effect immediately and needs no restart — the host, username and password stay in .env because they're credentials.
