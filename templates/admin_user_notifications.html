Tells the person who asked, rather than only telling you: a reply to their problem report, their report becoming an incident, something they requested arriving, or maintenance they asked to hear about. What each person wants and where to reach them is theirs to set on their own account page — this switch only decides whether any of it happens at all.

Switch

Off by default. Turning it on makes the portal message your users, so it's a deliberate choice — and nothing is sent to anyone who hasn't entered contact details of their own.

Off by default. Seerr already emails its own users directly when something they requested is approved, declined, or becomes available — leaving this off avoids a duplicate. This only suppresses the email copy of Seerr-sourced events; Discord DMs for the same events are unaffected.

Default notification settings

The baseline a user starts with if they've never saved their own notification preferences. Changing and saving this here never touches anyone who already has — for that, use "Apply to all users" on the setting you want to force, which immediately overwrites every existing user's own choice for that one setting. Default is quiet and forward-looking; Override is loud and immediate.
